Webinar: Using a Theory of Change to Learn and Share Lessons Across Sites

USAID’s Conservation Enterprises Learning Group facilitates learning by using a generalized theory of change to help teams monitor, evaluate, and share lessons from their conservation enterprise approaches.

In 2021, USAID and Measuring Impact II established an Impact Lab—a series of five facilitated conversations around the theory of change. Teams from around the world shared insights on how to monitor and evaluate outcomes and address common challenges. In this webinar, the Impact Lab participants presented case studies on how they use the CE theory of change to map their project assumptions, monitoring strategies, and lessons learned.
